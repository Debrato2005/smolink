# Surface: Smolink public home and app shell

Scope: `/` (Persuade), plus shared shell. Workspace and auth inherit the world at controlled and medium intensity (Operate).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A neobrutalist product page built from the user's three references, with the shortener as the hero action. It refuses a decorative hero that hides the task, and it refuses demo or sample wording.

OWN-WORLD: Sky ground with a 64px SVG grid. Paper surfaces with 3px ink borders and zero-blur offsets. Yellow for emphasis and navigation state, ink for the primary inverse buttons, solid accent fills on feature cards.

STORY: The visitor reads one headline, pastes a link, presses Shorten, and gets a ticket that states the characters cut. Below, true highlights, four features (two marked coming soon in production), three steps, and questions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered two-line display headline with "Smol" in outlined yellow, a short lede, then the shortener card. The URL field and Shorten button stay inside the first viewport at 390 x 844.

FORM: User-pinned references (neobrutalism.dev, neubrutalism.com, Medium guide). Concept roll skipped. Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
